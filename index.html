04 / HOW TO READ THIS

Compare roles, not reputations.

Position groups use the longest time in each tactical role. A replacement inherits the outgoing player’s role until the next recorded tactical shift. Broad groups simplify hybrid roles.

Understand the measures.

Non-penalty xG sums StatsBomb’s shot-quality estimates, excluding penalties. Shot xA sums the xG of shots explicitly linked to a player’s passes; it is not a separately trained expected-assist model.

Keep the denominator.

Progressive passes are completed passes gaining at least 15 of 120 pitch units, ending in the attacking half; set pieces are included. Pressures count recorded actions, not successful ball wins.

A tournament snapshot, not a scouting verdict.

Knockout progression creates unequal playing opportunities. Opponent strength, team possession and game state are not adjusted. Small samples and tactical differences matter. No overall ability score or future-performance prediction is produced.

Raw event files remain with the provider. This demo publishes aggregate research, including coarse zone patterns, with source attribution. It is intended for noncommercial analysis.
